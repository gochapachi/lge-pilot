# Outreach Scripts — copy → change line 1 → send

## Click-to-WhatsApp ad (Meta Ads Manager — zero ban risk)
**Objective:** Engagement → Messaging apps → WhatsApp · **Budget:** ₹200–300/day · **Audience:** Kanpur, 25–60, "Small business owners"/"Business page admins" behaviors
**Primary text:** "Har din Google pe aane wale reviews ka jawab, naye blogs, naye patients — sab automatic. Aapka time zero. ₹15,000 trial month me pura system setup karke dete hain. 👇 Message karein"
**Headline:** Google Reviews + SEO — Fully Automatic | **CTA button:** Send WhatsApp Message
**Creative:** 15-sec screen-record demo (review gate → AI reply → blog). No stock footage.

## Opt-out line for warm/follow-up messages (reduces block reports)
> "Agar ye aapke kaam ka nahi hai, bas 'no' likh dein — main aage message nahi karunga 🙏"

## Anti-spam rules (this keeps your number alive)
- Personalize line 1 with something real you saw (their listing, a post, a review).
- Manual sends only. 30–60 per day per number. No bulk-sender tools.
- Max 3 touches per prospect. Any "no" → reply politely and stop.
- Keep WhatsApp messages under ~6 lines.

## Demo call script (10 minutes)
1. **(2m)** "How do most patients/customers find you today?" — listen, don't pitch.
2. **(2m)** Pull up THEIR Google listing on screen, next to a top competitor. Show the gap: unreplied reviews, one bad review visible at top, no posts.
3. **(3m)** Demo: review gate + AI reply + one sample blog → Google post → carousel. One sentence: "Ye sab automatic chalta hai."
4. **(2m)** Offer: *founding-client — ₹0 setup if you start this week.* Show the tier table (Basic/₹4,999, Growth ₹9,999, Pro ₹19,999).
5. **(1m)** Close: "We start with ₹2,999 this week — website live in 48h, review system in 7 days. Payment link bhej doon?"

## Objection handlers
- **"I already have a website guy."** → "Perfect, keep him — he does the site, we do everything he doesn't: review replies, SEO content, Google posts, lead routing. You can even keep your site."
- **"Too expensive."** → "One new patient/customer a month more than covers it. Basic is ₹4,999 — less than a month of newspaper ads, and it works every day."
- **"I'll think about it."** → "Of course. I'll hold the ₹0 founding setup till tomorrow evening. Demo is in your WhatsApp — 2 minutes."
- **"I have no time for this."** → "That's exactly why it exists — one 30-min setup call, then I do everything. You only approve what you want to."
- **"Does it actually work?"** → "<Dental client> in Kanpur: <real numbers — X new reviews, Y review average>. I'll connect you directly with them if you want."
