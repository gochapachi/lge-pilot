# GTM Playbook — "Local Growth Engine" (n8n automation for service businesses)

## 0. Straight talk first
- 20 clients TODAY is not a real number. Today's real job: assets live + 60–100 prospects contacted + 3–5 demo calls booked + first money in.
- Realistic path: **3–6 pilots this week → 20 total clients in 10–14 days** at 80–100 contacts/day.
- You close with **proof + speed**, not with a pretty website. Do NOT rebuild your WordPress site first. Sell first, polish later.

## 1. The Offer (call it this everywhere)
**"Local Growth Engine"** — *Your website + Google reviews + SEO fully automated in 48 hours. Zero staff time.*
- Website from logo + business details + 1 prompt; owner edits via simple form (no tech)
- Every lead from the site → WhatsApp/email/CRM wherever the owner wants
- Review gate: bad reviews come to the owner privately, good reviews get AI-polished text + direct link to post on Google
- AI replies to every Google review same-day
- 4 SEO blogs/month (keyword + competitor researched) → each becomes a Google post, 1 carousel, 1 lead-magnet infographic, 1 animated video
- Review booster: branded placard images sent to customers' WhatsApp asking for reviews

## 4. Funnel math (why daily volume is the whole game)
100 personalized contacts → 20–30 replies → 8–12 calls → 2–4 closes.
- 80–100 contacts/day ≈ 60–90 focused minutes with saved templates (copy → change line 1 → send).
- 20 clients ≈ roughly 2 weeks at this pace. Missing days = missing revenue.

## 5. Channels (in order)
### 5a. INBOUND-FIRST (zero ban risk — run these permanently)
- **Referral engine:** 2 dental clients × 3 intros × ₹1,000 credit per close ≈ 6 warm leads who EXPECT your message. Warm WA messages are not cold outreach — near-zero risk, 5–10x close rate. Highest ROI tactic you own. Repeat with every new client.
- **Click-to-WhatsApp ads (the real scale cheat):** Meta LOVES it when you pay them — zero ban risk. ₹200–300/day, target: Kanpur 25–60, interests: small business owners; creative: 15-sec demo video; CTA: "Send WhatsApp Message". At ₹5–15/conversation in India, ₹3,000 gets ~30–80 warm leads. Try Meta's free ad credits first. This out-scales any cold sending.
- **WhatsApp Status marketing:** post one demo/result daily as Status → every saved contact sees it → inbound "kya price hai?" DMs. Underused, free, unlimited.
- **Local WhatsApp business groups** (Kanpur traders/CA/coaching groups): be useful, answer questions, people message YOU.
- **wa.me links everywhere:** Google Business Profile chat, email signature, Instagram bio, QR on visiting cards shared at clinic partners.
